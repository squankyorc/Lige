THE IDEA

One rule. Endless possibilities.

Color changes only when you make a move.

01 / CHOOSE

Bring a little color.

Pick your player’s RGB color. The world stays black until you take a step or deliberately paint a voxel.

02 / WALK

Leave something behind.

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows. Your chosen color joins the average of the block beneath your new position.

03 / EXPLORE

Watch a rule unfold.

Every block averages itself with its six face neighbors, all at once. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and click a voxel or enter its coordinates to paint.
